# Aero Ability Review — 2026-08-21

## Audit basis and runtime key

Sources reviewed: `src/main/resources/data/classes/aero.json`, `src/main/resources/data/styles/aero_styles.json`, `src/main/resources/data/ability-visual-manifest.json`, the Aero/Corruptus EntityEffect JSONs under `src/main/resources/Server/Entity/Effects/MOTM/`, `GameplayPlaybackManager.java`, `AbilityRuntimeFamily.java`, `AbilityRuntimeEffects.java`, `AbilityRuntimeMath.java`, the projectile/field/dash/transformation adapters, and the canon documents `docs/ABILITY_REFERENCE.md`, `docs/style-ability-intent-canon-2026-06-01.md`, and `docs/intent-canon-visual-fitness-2026-07-16.md`.

Runtime-family shorthand is the resolver in `runtime/ability/AbilityRuntimeFamily.java`: projectile (`projectile`, `projectile_line`, `projectile_volley`, `wave_line`, `chain`), field (`ground_zone`, `support_zone`, `barrier`, `channel`), movement (`dash`, `dash_strike`, `leap`, `dive_strike`, `air_stall`), transformation, and follow-up/instant families. `GameplayPlaybackManager.executeAbility` runs playback, projectile launch, fields, combat, target effects, and transformations; `playAbility` applies a generic cast effect unless movement owns the visual, and the dash adapter owns cast/travel/impact cues for dashes.

The manifest's `legacy: true` rows use raw vanilla spawner paths. `HytaleAssetResolver` preserves those paths for asset summaries, but `AbilityRuntimeEffects.asRuntimeEffectId` accepts only `MOTM_*`/EntityEffect IDs; raw paths therefore do not become live EntityEffects. In practice the generic class/family fallback is used (or no field loop/proxy visual is created), while the manifest still documents an unauthored vanilla fallback. This is reported explicitly below. The Scream style is an important exception: themed overrides in `AbilityRuntimeEffects` select `MOTM_Aero_Scream_*` despite its legacy manifest rows.

## Base Class — Aero

* **Element/theme/colors:** element is “Speed, lightning, evasion”; theme is Air; description is the Storm Dancer fantasy (wind mobility plus lightning burst). Primary `#9370DB` (medium purple), secondary `#FFD700` (gold), accent `#E6E6FA` (lavender). This matches the canon palette `#9370DB/#FFD700/#E6E6FA` and the intended skirmisher/assassin identity.
* **Passive — Wind Walker:** data says +25% movement speed and +80% native Hytale energy, with no class resource and no duplicate vertical movement. `ClassPassiveManager.tickAeroPassive` reapplies the signature-energy max modifier multiplicatively at `1.80` and snapshots/multiplies base, walk/run/sprint, strafe and acceleration settings by `1.25`; it removes the modifier and restores the movement snapshot on class/entity cleanup. This matches the concept and the canon’s deliberate data-only/no-vertical-visual rule. It is implemented as a native-stat modifier, not a custom Aero resource.
* **Stats/resistances/playstyle:** starts health 85, mana 100, stamina 120, armor 6, magic resist 8, attack damage 14, ability power 12, movement speed 1.15, health regen 1.2, mana regen 1.8; per-level growth health 4, mana 5, stamina 6, armor 0.4, magic resist 0.6, attack damage 1.1, ability power 0.9, health regen 0.08, mana regen 0.12. Innate lightning resistance +20%, air +15%, physical -5%, earth -10%. Tags are assassin/speed/burst/evasion/skirmisher; recommended for advanced hit-and-run players; synergies are lightning/wind/speed/storm/evasion.
* **Identity verdict:** MATCH at base-class level. Fast movement, lightning resistance/affinity, low durability, and no resource cost are coherent. The principal class-level weakness is not the passive but visual consistency: most styles fall back to generic wind/dagger/vanilla assets instead of the declared purple/gold/lavender Aero identity.

**Final table count (30 rows): MATCH 6, PARTIAL 13, MISMATCH 11.** Legacy rows: **16/30** (`legacy:true`); authored rows: **14/30**. The 14 authored rows are `smite`, `chain_lightning`, `twister`, `funnel_cloud`, `eye_of_the_storm`, `air_slash`, `gale_cutter`, `razor_wind`, `air_shot`, `bullet_storm`, `pressure_burst`, `smog`, `toxic_breath`, `acid_rain`. (Scream, dash, and Smoke Form have useful code-level themed/runtime overrides despite legacy manifest flags.)

### Top 8 issues, ranked

1. **Legacy visual coverage is still 16/30 for Aero.** Raw vanilla spawner paths are not live EntityEffect IDs; generic fallback or no loop/field visual is used. This is most damaging for Jet, Jump, Smoke, Gale Wizard, and Tempest.
2. **Dash/mobility abilities do not implement their defining contact mechanics.** Jet Burst never displaces enemies passed, Afterburner does not burn trail contacts, Divebomb does not enforce airborne/knockback impact, and Leap immediately launches rather than arming/landing.
3. **Wind Blade visuals violate locked canon.** Air Slash/Gale Cutter/Razor Wind all use the same blue-white Tornado model and wind stack; there are no pale-yellow arcs, X-crossing slashes, or five distinct slash silhouettes.
4. **Most Aero projectiles use generic Tornado/Spark_Living-style surrogates.** Pressure projectiles read as tornadoes, not compression rings/bullets; field proxies ignore manifest model metadata and resolve to generic field roles.
5. **Pressure Burst has no charge state or growth.** Current `projectile` launches immediately; the canon requires up to 4s hold-charge, increasing damage/size, and a visibly growing shot.
6. **Aero protection/stealth forms are numerically and visually off-canon.** Smoke Form lacks the canon-locked 50% projectile DR and uses `ModelChange: Bat` instead of a smoky humanoid; Cyclone Shield lacks projectile-specific reduction and its cyclone loop is legacy/unresolved.
7. **Scream/Battle Cry support semantics are wrong.** Battle Cry only buffs the caster, applies +20% attack/+25% speed rather than current +15%/+10%, has conflicting duration/canon values, and has no persistent ally aura because its legacy loop is rejected.
8. **Thunder/Tornado/Tempest control cues are incomplete.** Thunderclap falls back to generic wind rather than Spirit_Thunder/lightning, Twister loses knockback/lift because `knockback` is not generic field-routed, and Tempest applies pull/stun/damage but not slow and has no persistent visual.

### Systemic patterns

* The code’s runtime family split is strong: projectiles, fields, movement, and transformation are distinct and generally observable. Native projectile configs and chain state are particularly solid.
* The visual resolver is the bottleneck: only Scream has style-specific override effects, while most Aero rows converge on generic Aero wind/dagger effects or raw vanilla paths. EntityEffect colors/sounds are often sensible in isolation but not action-specific.
* Data, canon, and implementation drift in several current rows: `eye_of_the_storm`, `leap`, `razor_wind`, `smoke_bomb`, `smoke_form`, and `pressure_burst` have materially different descriptions or cast types across the style JSON, reference canon, and runtime.
* Aero’s class passive is intentionally data/stat-only and is the coherent part of the class. It should remain separate from style vertical movement; the audit found no Wind Walker duplication of jumps/dashes.
