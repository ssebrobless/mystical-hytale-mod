# Corruptus Ability Review — 2026-08-21

## Audit basis

Reviewed the complete Corruptus class, all 10 styles and 30 abilities, the visual manifest, Corruptus EntityEffects, playback dispatch, and the projectile/field/summon/channel/control/transformation families. Primary sources are `src/main/resources/data/classes/corruptus.json`, `src/main/resources/data/styles/corruptus_styles.json`, `src/main/resources/data/ability-visual-manifest.json`, `src/main/java/com/motm/manager/ClassPassiveManager.java`, `src/main/java/com/motm/manager/GameplayPlaybackManager.java`, `src/main/java/com/motm/runtime/ability/**`, and `src/main/java/com/motm/util/HytaleAssetResolver.java`. Intent cross-checks use `docs/style-ability-intent-canon-2026-06-01.md` and `docs/intent-canon-visual-fitness-2026-07-16.md`.

**Runtime/manifest caveat used throughout.** `HytaleAssetResolver.resolve()` returns manifest values verbatim. `applyEffectById()` only resolves an `EntityEffect` asset, while `AbilityRuntimeEffects.asRuntimeEffectId()` accepts `MOTM_*`/EntityEffect IDs and rejects raw particle-spawner/system paths. Consequently, every row marked `legacy=true` below is an unauthored vanilla fallback in the manifest and is a concept/visual gap even where a class/style fallback or a separate runtime adapter happens to provide some authored effect. The generic Corruptus fallbacks are `MOTM_Corruptus_Cast`, `MOTM_Corruptus_Impact`, and `MOTM_Corruptus_Move`; Void and Shadow styles are specially mapped to the authored Void family.

## Base Class — Corruptus

- **Element/theme/colors:** element `Corruption, flames, damage`; theme `Dark Magic + Fire`; primary `#8B0000`, secondary `#4B0082`, accent `#FF4500`. The class description explicitly frames a high-risk sacrifice-for-destruction identity.
- **Passive concept:** `Soul Harvest`: hostile kills build up to 5 stacks; each stack grants +2% damage and 1% damage reduction; at five stacks lethal damage restores 50% max HP, clears stacks, and starts a 600-second lockout; Corruptus uses cooldowns rather than Souls.
- **Passive code behavior:** `ClassPassiveManager.onMobKilled()` increments a Corruptus stack up to five and blocks gains during lockout. `getAbilityDamageModifier()` exposes the stack damage bonus; incoming damage is reduced by the stack reduction. When incoming damage is at least current health and five stacks are present, the manager restores 50% max HP, clears stacks, sets a 10-minute tick lockout, applies generic `MOTM_Corruptus_Impact`, and removes BURN/DOT. This is wired into `handleIncomingPlayerDamage()` rather than normal death.
- **Does it match?** **PARTIAL.** The stack math, five-stack gate, 50% restore, clear, 600-second lockout, stat bonuses, and cooldown/no-Soul resource model match the class JSON. The kill hook does not verify that the killed mob was hostile; it increments for any qualifying mob-kill callback. Resurrection feedback is generic `MOTM_Corruptus_Impact`, not a dedicated dark-resurrection/Soul Harvest effect. Also, the passive is not itself visible at stack gain time beyond logs/state summaries.
- **Stats/resistances/playstyle:** starting HP 90, mana 110, stamina 85, armor 5, magic resist 5, attack 10, ability power 16, movement 1.0, health regen 1.0, mana regen 2.0; per-level growth HP +4, mana +6, stamina +3, armor +0.3, magic resist +0.4, attack +0.7, ability power +1.5, health regen +0.06, mana regen +0.15. Innate fire 15%, dark 20%, corruption 25%; holy -15%, water -10%. Tags are damage/burst/DoT/glass-cannon/high-risk; difficulty expert; recommended for players who want overwhelming offense and risk management; synergies fire/dark/corruption/chaos/destruction.
- **Identity coherence verdict:** **PARTIAL but coherent.** Dark-fire palettes, offensive stats, and a high-risk resurrection passive reinforce the fantasy. The unvalidated hostile-kill boundary and generic resurrection visual are load-bearing polish gaps.

## Summary

### Counts

- **MATCH:** 1 (`rift`)
- **PARTIAL:** 14 (`fireball`, `ignite`, `raise_dead`, `shadow_step`, `umbral_veil`, `hellfire`, `infernal_ground`, `soul_scorch`, `imbue_power`, `void_spawn`, `scarak_egg`, `pterodactyl_form`, `triceratops_form`, `t_rex_form`)
- **MISMATCH:** 15 (`combust`, `life_drain`, `death_mark`, `dark_embrace`, `dominate`, `mind_shatter`, `hivemind`, `imbue_fortitude`, `imbue_swiftness`, `sanctuary`, `absorb`, `purify`, `consume`, `brood_surge`, `locust_queen`)
- **Manifest visual coverage:** 5 authored/non-legacy rows (`fireball`, `hellfire`, `infernal_ground`, `soul_scorch`, `rift`); **25 legacy/unauthored rows**. The five authored rows share families, but Fire/HellFlame cast routing is not consistently selected by `AbilityRuntimeEffects`.

### Top 8 issues (ranked)

1. **Legacy visual coverage is the dominant systemic defect:** 25/30 Corruptus rows point at raw vanilla spawner/system paths. They do not resolve as EntityEffects, so most abilities have no authored per-style visual, sound, tint, or mechanic-specific cue.
2. **Mentokinesis control contract is wrong:** `dominate`/`hivemind` are clamped to a 15–20s window; recast refreshes rather than toggles/release, `hivemind` is 15s minimum instead of 6s, and `mind_shatter` does not center/detonate controlled allies. The pink marker is a useful supplemental effect but not a complete ability visual.
3. **Atonement is functionally inverted/missing:** Sanctuary does not heal/protect friendlies in its zone, Absorb has no shield because `shield_percent` is absent, and Purify is `self_burst` with zero damage and no cleanse (cleanse only runs for cast type `cleanse`). All three have legacy Void visuals rather than white/gold corrupted-holy effects.
4. **Execute/consumption mechanics are incomplete:** Combust only gets a burn-present ×1.75 modifier and has no dedicated burn-consumption presentation; Consume has no below-10% auto-kill (uses <=35%/<=18% modifiers) and only generic 20% lifesteal.
5. **Fire/Hell Flame cast routing bypasses authored cast effects:** `AbilityRuntimeEffects.castEffectId()` has no Fire/HellFlame branch, so Fireball and Hellfire caster casts can use generic purple/void `MOTM_Corruptus_Cast` even though their manifest rows are authored fire/blue-fire. Impact/field paths are more reliable.
6. **Life Drain healing is over/under-specified:** channel pulses heal 45% and the `lifesteal` token adds a separate 20% status, rather than the stated 50% of actual damage; the row has no authored tether/drain EntityEffect.
7. **Scarak runtime identity and surge behavior are wrong:** Egg uses a delayed three-summon spec but duplicates Fighter instead of Defender and does not place `Deco_Scarak_Eggsacks`; Brood Surge neither instantly awakens eggs nor grants +40% movement; Locust Queen uses `Scarak_Fighter` rather than `Scarak_Broodmother`.
8. **Self-buff/stance semantics drift:** Imbuements do not enforce one exclusive stance, Fortitude has immediate heal/defense rather than a shield/HoT, Swiftness has no movement-speed token (its dash fields are ignored for `self_buff`), and Power/Swiftness arm undocumented weapon follow-ups.

### Systemic patterns

- **Manifest/runtime split:** manifest data is descriptive, but generic runtime resolvers often bypass manifest cast IDs; legacy filtering is silent except for warnings, and special style mappings (Shadow/Void, transformation adapters, control marker) create inconsistent actual visuals.
- **Visuals are not mechanically coupled:** field support/heal, psychic center selection, death-trigger explosions, summon identity, and execute thresholds are decided by separate runtime families; a valid `cast_type` alone does not guarantee the intended target or lifecycle behavior.
- **Color/theme drift:** authored blue HellFlame/Void effects are strongest where explicitly themed; legacy and generic fallback rows collapse Fire, Necro, Mentokinesis, Imbuement, Atonement, Scarak, and Primordial into purple/teal void smoke. This is especially damaging for bright-pink Mentokinesis and white/gold Atonement.
- **Catalog/orphan signals:** Acid EntityEffects exist but are not used by these Corruptus rows; Control Marker exists and is used only as supplemental control feedback. The presence of authored assets does not imply manifest or playback wiring.
