## Audit basis and runtime interpretation

Sources reviewed were `src/main/resources/data/classes/terra.json`, `src/main/java/com/motm/manager/ClassPassiveManager.java`, `src/main/resources/data/styles/terra_styles.json`, `src/main/resources/data/ability-visual-manifest.json`, the referenced authored EntityEffects under `src/main/resources/Server/Entity/Effects/MOTM/`, `GameplayPlaybackManager.java`, `AbilityRuntimeFamily.java`, and the terrain/field/projectile/dash/follow-up/specific runtime adapters. `GameplayPlaybackManager.executeAbility` invokes projectile, persistent-field, supplemental-terrain, specific, combat, target-effect, channel, transformation, summon and follow-up stages (manager lines 1668-1805). The family classifier maps projectile cast types to PROJECTILE, ground zones/barriers/support zones to FIELD, ground bursts/strikes/targets to TERRAIN, dashes to MOVEMENT, and self-buff/gaze/line-control to FOLLOW_UP (`src/main/java/com/motm/runtime/ability/AbilityRuntimeFamily.java:16-57`).

The manifest has 30 Terra rows: 24 with `legacy: true` and six authored rows with no legacy flag (`magma_sling`, `rubble_rouser`, `debris`, `sandstorm`, `dust_devil`, `vitrification`). Per the audit contract, a legacy row is treated as an unauthored visual fallback even when a row also contains an MOTM-looking ID: raw particle-spawner paths are not EntityEffect assets. The runtime confirms why this is a gap: `GameplayPlaybackManager.applyEffectById` resolves only `EntityEffect.getAssetMap().getAsset(effectId)` and returns false when the raw spawner path is not an EntityEffect (`GameplayPlaybackManager.java:2770-2795`).

## Base Class — Terra

* **Element/theme/colors:** element `Stone, nature, defense`; theme `Earth`; primary `#8B4513` (saddlebrown), secondary `#228B22` (forest green), accent `#CD853F` (peru). The identity is explicitly stone/soil resilience, terrain control and endurance (`src/main/resources/data/classes/terra.json:2-10`).
* **Passive concept and code behavior:** `terra_passive_immovable` (“Immovable”) promises 20% less incoming knockback, 1% max-health regeneration per second below 30% HP, 50% faster pickaxe mining, and cave vision. `ClassPassiveManager.getIncomingKnockbackMultiplier` implements 0.80 and `getMiningDamageMultiplier` implements 1.50 for item IDs containing `pickaxe`/`_pick` (`ClassPassiveManager.java:279-306`). `tickTerraPassive` resets stationary state on movement, increments it while still, heals at the 20-tick interval below the configured threshold, and scans for cave vision (`566-599`). The cave-light implementation intentionally removes/does not add dynamic light, so cave vision is currently HUD/mechanical only (`891-921`). The loader also reads a `conditional_shield` condition for stationary ticks, but Terra JSON has no such effect; it therefore falls back to two seconds (`1363-1372`), and the stationary shield/primed state is not actually granted by `tickTerraPassive`.
* **Stats/resistances/playstyle:** starts at 120 HP, 80 mana, 100 stamina, 15 armor, 10 magic resist, 12 attack, 8 ability power, 1.0 movement, 2.5 health regen and 1.0 mana regen; per level +8 HP, +3 mana, +4 stamina, +1.2 armor, +0.8 magic resist, +0.8 attack, +0.5 ability power, +0.15 health regen and +0.05 mana regen. Innate resistances are physical +5%, earth +15%, nature +10%, fire -5%. Tags are tank/defense/sustain/control; recommendation is front-line damage soaking/protection; difficulty beginner; synergies earth/nature/stone/defense/fortification.
* **Identity coherence verdict:** **PARTIAL.** The stat package, earth/nature palette, knockback resistance, sustain and terrain-oriented styles are coherent. Cave vision is not visibly implemented, and the passive's implied stationary shield is data/code-incoherent (loader expects an absent condition and tick code never primes it), weakening the “unmovable guardian” promise.

## Summary

* **Verdict counts:** MATCH **3**; PARTIAL **16**; MISMATCH **11** (30 abilities total).
* **Manifest coverage:** **24 legacy/unauthored-visual rows**, **6 authored rows**. Authored: `magma_sling`, `rubble_rouser`, `debris`, `sandstorm`, `dust_devil`, `vitrification`.
* **Top 8 issues, ranked:**
  1. **Legacy visual coverage is systemic:** 24/30 Terra rows still send one or more raw vanilla spawner paths; `applyEffectById` accepts EntityEffect assets only, so most cast/travel/impact/loop phases are missing at runtime. This affects every style and prevents otherwise-correct mechanics from being MATCH.
  2. **Sinkhole damage is catastrophically scaled:** `dot_percent_per_second: 2` is passed as a fraction of max HP (`health.max * 2.0`) rather than 0.02, so suffocation can exceed the target's full health per pulse (`FieldSinkholeHytaleAdapter.java:72-89,118-144`).
  3. **Defensive percentage tokens do not match authored concepts:** Metal Coat promises 50% reduction but `defense_buff` is fixed at 20%; Obsidian Skin's `damage_reduction` is not a recognized caster token; Gargoyle's `damage_reduction` and `untargetable` are also ignored.
  4. **Several signature control/terrain mechanics are data-only:** Pillar Strike never places its pillar or launches; Tunnel never burrows/knocks up or grants evasion; Gargoyle never locks/invulnerably transforms; Rubble Rouser never arms its described follow-up.
  5. **Support promises are not wired to allies:** Lava Pool does not protect caster/allies, and Refraction's support field does not grant nearby allies its listed shield/heal/damage buff. Field support pulses are owner-centric and the crystal-refraction terrain effect has no branch.
  6. **Unrecognized effect tokens silently erase concepts:** `sand_empower`, `lure`, `damage_reduction`, `untargetable` and several other data tokens are not in the caster/target token policy, leaving Vitrification, Sapling/Nightshade lure, Obsidian/Gargoyle reduction and Refraction's support behavior incomplete.
  7. **Authored visual reuse is often semantically wrong:** Debris uses ModelVFX `Dagger_Dash` and Quake's golem-stomp sound; Vitrification uses Rubble model and Sand Cast as travel; Magma Sling uses `PrototypeBlockPlaceSuccess` as travel ModelVFX. These are concrete concept↔asset fitness defects even in nonlegacy rows.
  8. **Terra passive is internally inconsistent:** cave vision intentionally has no visible light/fullbright path, while loader logic expects an absent `conditional_shield` condition to derive stationary ticks; `tickTerraPassive` never primes/applies the implied stationary shield. The resulting passive is less than the class JSON promises.

**Systemic pattern:** runtime infrastructure is strongest for generic projectile/field/dash primitives and a few explicit special cases (Magma Sling, Dust Devil, Lapidary, Sinkhole, Alloy), but style-specific semantics are not data-driven end-to-end. Unrecognized effect tokens, missing terrain-spec branches, generic target filtering, and legacy manifest rows repeatedly turn distinctive Terra concepts into generic damage/status or no-op casts.
